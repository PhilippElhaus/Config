# Reports

- Keep recurring reports in a consistent format. Start with the overall
  result, then show the exceptions and what needs attention. Make each
  item short enough to scan on a phone.
- Group repeated alerts by cause and show a count. Put full diagnostic
  detail in a log or a linked breakdown. Use a few consistent symbols
  for status in chat reports.
- Use compact comparison tables when they make a decision easier.
  Include the result and the evidence that changes the decision.
