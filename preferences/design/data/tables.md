# Tables

- Give similar comparison columns equal widths. Keep counters, selection
  controls, and short identifiers narrow. Give previews and descriptive
  fields the room they need.
- Put related measures next to each other. Keep comparable rows and
  columns aligned so I can compare them without moving between panels.
- Keep long tables within the page even when column groups are expanded.
  Use concise headers, compact spacing, and deliberate wrapping. Keep
  short headers on one line where they remain readable.
- Keep table headers and frequently used filters visible while scrolling
  long views. Confine horizontal scrolling to the row or panel that needs
  it when a wide comparison cannot fit.
